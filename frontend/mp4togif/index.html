Select Photo to convert a picture into a still GIF, or MP4 video to create a looping animation.

Upload an MP4 containing a video track. The first 30 seconds are converted at 10 frames per second, with both dimensions limited to 480 pixels while preserving the aspect ratio. Shorter videos are converted in full. GIFs loop continuously and contain no audio.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose an MP4 video and accept the privacy policy.
  2. Click Convert to GIF and wait for processing.
  3. Save the animated GIF using the download link.

Limits

Uploads may be up to 200 MiB and two hours long. Only the first 30 seconds appear in the GIF. Output files are limited to 50 MiB, and processing can take up to 10 minutes.
