Convert MP4 video to MP3 audio

Save the audio from an MP4 recording without keeping the video picture. The tool extracts the first audio track and encodes it as a 192 kbps MP3 that you can download.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose an MP4 file containing an audio track and accept the privacy policy.
  2. Click Extract audio and wait for processing to finish.
  3. Download the MP3 file using the link below the form.

What are the audio extraction limits?

Upload an MP4 up to 200 MiB and two hours long, with no more than 10 streams. Processing can take up to 10 minutes. Silent videos cannot produce an MP3. Encoding at 192 kbps does not improve the quality of the original recording.

Download availability and privacy

Result links are available for 7 days. Files then move into ZIP archives deleted 30 days after creation. File-related IP addresses and download times are recorded for abuse investigation. Read the privacy policy before submitting files or links.
