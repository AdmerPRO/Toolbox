Save the audio from YouTube, Instagram or TikTok

Prepare an MP3 from a YouTube video, Instagram Reel or video post, or TikTok video when you own the content or have permission to download it. Choose 128, 192, 256 or 320 kbps for the output audio.

How to use this tool

  1. Accept the privacy policy and paste the permitted video link.
  2. Load the video information and select an MP3 bitrate.
  3. Start the audio download preparation and save the resulting MP3.

Which MP3 bitrate should I choose?

A higher bitrate generally produces a larger file. It cannot restore detail missing from the original audio, so choosing 320 kbps does not guarantee better sound than the source. This tool prepares one video at a time rather than a playlist.
