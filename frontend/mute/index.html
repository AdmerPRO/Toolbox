Make a silent MP4 video

Remove speech, music or background noise from an MP4 before sharing it. The tool copies the first video track without re-encoding and leaves out the audio tracks.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose an MP4 containing a video track and accept the privacy policy.
  2. Click Mute video and wait for the result.
  3. Download the silent MP4 using the result link.

Does muting change the picture quality?

The selected video track is copied without re-encoding, so muting does not introduce another round of picture compression. Uploads must be at most 200 MiB and two hours long, with no more than 10 streams. Processing can take up to 10 minutes.

Download availability and privacy

Result links are available for 7 days. Files then move into ZIP archives deleted 30 days after creation. File-related IP addresses and download times are recorded for abuse investigation. Read the privacy policy before submitting files or links.
